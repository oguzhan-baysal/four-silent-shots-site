Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

What this is

Four Silent Shots is a YouTube channel. The software described here is the channel's own upload tool. It is used by the channel owner only, to publish the channel's own videos.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from viewers. The tool does not read viewer information, comments, or analytics of third parties. It authenticates with the channel owner's own Google account through OAuth and uses only the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes, limited to the channel's own videos.

What Google receives

When the channel owner authorises the tool, Google processes the OAuth consent as described in Google's own privacy policy. We store only the resulting OAuth refresh token, on hardware controlled by the channel owner.

Third parties

No data is sold or shared. Videos are uploaded to YouTube, which is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token is deleted with it.
